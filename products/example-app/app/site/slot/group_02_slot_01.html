Lower Saddle

Ridge Loop, entry 1 of 2. Status: seeded.

Leaves the coast path at the second stile and climbs to the saddle on a grass track that is rutted in places.

What to look for

  • The bay comes into view all at once at the top of the rise.
  • Drystone field boundaries that predate the current path.

Accessibility

Sustained climb with one stile; no wheeled access.

Shortest route to a view of the whole bay, at the cost of the only real ascent in the guide.
