Harbour Wall

Coast Path, entry 1 of 4. Status: seeded.

Begins at the harbour car park and follows the wall east for roughly 400 metres. Flat paving throughout, with no steps.

What to look for

  • Barnacle bands on the lower stonework, which mark the usual tide range.
  • Iron mooring rings set into the wall at irregular intervals.
  • Wind-shaped scrub on the landward side.

Accessibility

Level throughout; suitable for wheeled access as far as the beacon.

A good orientation stop before the rest of the group, since the whole bay is visible from the end of the wall.
