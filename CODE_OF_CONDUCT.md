# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be straightforward and civil. Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith.
Criticize work, not people.

## What is expected

- Keep discussion technical and specific. "This breaks when the catalog is empty" is useful;
  "this is bad" is not.
- Accept that maintenance here is intermittent. Following up is fine; escalating tone because a
  response was slow is not.
- Respect the project's stated scope. Disagreeing with a design decision is welcome; relitigating
  a documented non-goal repeatedly is not.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments about a person or group.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome personal attention.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or deliberately derailing threads.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages, and any other project space, and to
conduct elsewhere that is directed at people because of their participation here.

## Reporting

This project is maintained by one person, and no separate contact address is published for
conduct reports.

- For behavior by another participant, use **GitHub's own reporting tools**. From any comment
  or profile, use *Report content* or *Report abuse*. GitHub's staff can act on account-level
  behavior in ways an individual maintainer cannot, and their
  [Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines)
  apply here regardless of this document.
- For a concern about the maintainer's own conduct, open an issue if you are comfortable doing
  so, or use GitHub's reporting tools if you are not.

Security vulnerabilities are a separate matter and should **not** be reported publicly. See
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Reports that reach the maintainer will be read and acted on as time allows. Possible responses
include editing or deleting comments, locking a thread, closing a contribution without review,
and blocking an account. Given the maintenance posture of this project, expect that action may
be blunt and quick rather than graduated — there is no capacity here for a mediation process.
